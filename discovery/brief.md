# Sprint 3: Find out why booth conversations go cold, then fix it

Due Fri, Nov 6, 12:00 PM AST. 20-hour budget. Opened Nov 2.

## The problem (as given)
Nadia Comeau, CEO of Sandbar Scheduling, spends about $9,000 on each of four trade shows a year and can't say what they bring in. Find out why, then build what fixes it. Write the problem in your own words before building.

- Client: Nadia Comeau (CEO). Team: Owen Gallant (sales), Erin Raman (operations).
- Interviews: one 30-min group call (all three), then two 15-min one-on-one follow-ups, timed by me. Files come when asked for.
- How often: four two-day shows a year.
- To find out: what they experience, negative impact (start with the $9,000), root cause (don't take the first answer), desired outcome (what Nadia wants to know 30 days after a show).

## Target
At Friday's live run, on a show the tool has never seen, at least 12 of 15 real conversations come out right.

## Done means
1. Interview transcripts + problem in my own words: who, what they experience, impact, how often, root cause, outcome.
2. Friday demo on a new show's files: at least 12/15 people right, and no one who shouldn't be emailed gets a draft.
3. It gives Nadia the 30-day answer she wants, for every real conversation.
4. Written plan: who looks after it, cost to run, what happens when it gets one wrong, what it can't fix.

## Hand-in
By Fri 12:00 mark sprint complete. One comment showing each done check, with transcripts, files and steps to run it.

## Out of scope
Sending emails, connecting to Sandbar's real inbox, contacts or booking page.

## Key lines from the ground rules
- A person is "right" when they appear ONCE, with the right PRIORITY and NEXT STEP, and their draft mentions SOMETHING SPECIFIC THEY SAID AT THE BOOTH.
- Personal or family details never go in a draft.
- "Priority" and "next step" are for me to find out.
- Friday: same file format, my own steps, no editing files or fixing output by hand.
- Plan as if the data were real: what's sent where, whether it's kept, what Nadia must approve.
- Interview link: private, not included here. Download each transcript.
