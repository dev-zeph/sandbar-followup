# Sandbar Scheduling: ground rules

- Use the files the client sends you. You don't need access to Sandbar's real systems.
- AI tools are fine. The data is made up. Plan as if it were real: what you'd send, where it goes, whether it's kept, and what Nadia would need to approve.
- The files are for building and testing. Check your work against them as you go.
- On Friday you run it live on a new show's files, in the same format. You can run your own steps. You can't change the files or fix the output by hand.
- A person is "right" when they appear once, with the right priority and next step, and their draft mentions something specific they said at the booth. Personal or family details never go in a draft. What "priority" and "next step" mean is for you to find out.
- Sending is not part of the target. The target is ready-to-send drafts.
- Your first interview is a 30-minute call with Nadia, Owen and Erin together. Each follow-up is 15 minutes with one of them. The clock starts at your first message. Use the follow-ups straight away or save them for when you hit something you don't know: that choice is yours, and we look at it. Plan your questions. If the client has to check something, the answer appears on your interview page.
